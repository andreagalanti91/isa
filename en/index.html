About me

Hi, I'm Isa 👋

Hi, I'm Isa, but to your pets I'll simply be “Tata Isa” (tata is Italian for nanny). I've loved animals all my life and I have two cats, Nito and Lola: they're my family and the love of my life. That's why I know how much it matters to leave the ones you love with someone you can trust.

🇮🇹 🇬🇧 🇪🇸 I speak Italian, English and Spanish.

FAQ

Any questions?

Which areas do you cover?

Florence, Chianti, Siena and Val d'Orcia. If you're elsewhere in Tuscany, message me and we'll see if I can reach you.

How much does it cost?

From €15 per hour. The final price depends on the service, duration and travel: message me on WhatsApp and I'll send you a tailored quote.

Is the first meeting free?

Yes, it's free and with no obligation. It's a chance to get to know each other: I learn about your pet and their habits, and you get to know me.

I'm on holiday: can you come to my hotel or agriturismo?

Yes. First check with your accommodation that your pet can stay in the room or apartment, then we'll agree on where and when.

Which animals do you look after?

Dogs and cats of every size and age. For other animals, message me and we'll talk about it.

Can you give medication?

Yes, following your vet's instructions. Please always leave them in writing.

Will you keep me updated?

Of course: I'll send you messages and photos on WhatsApp so you always know how they're doing.

What if there's an emergency?

I'll contact you straight away and, if needed, take your pet to your vet or the nearest veterinary clinic.

How far ahead should I book?

The sooner the better, especially for weddings and events. For last-minute requests, message me anyway: if I'm free, I'm there.

Which languages do you speak?

Italian, English and Spanish, which is my native language.
